What AutoChase does

AutoChase sits next to Jira so teams see SLA risk early and plan capacity against real open work — without leaving the tools they already use.

SLA, reminders & escalations

Track response and resolution targets, send ack/progress reminders, and escalate through your org hierarchy.

  • Dashboard & tracked issues
  • SLA rules & working calendar
  • Issue side panel
  • Leadership reports

Capacity planner

Compare open workload to weekly and sprint capacity so overload shows up before deadlines slip.

  • Sprint commitment
  • Backlog pressure
  • Availability & leave
  • Capacity exports
One Marketplace plan. Sites subscribe once, then a Jira admin turns Capacity and/or SLA on or off under Access → Products. There is no Standard vs Advanced edition lock in the app.

Access & who can open it

The AutoChase console is not open to every Jira user. Access is controlled by Jira admin rights and an optional delegate list.

WhoWhat they can do
Jira site administrators Full console. See the Access tab. Manage Products toggles and global managers.
Global managers Full console for projects, SLAs, capacity, and organisation. Cannot manage Access or Product toggles.
Everyone else Cannot use the console. They may still see the issue SLA panel when SLA is on and they can browse the issue.

Where to open AutoChase

  • Apps → AutoChase — main console for admins and global managers
  • Jira admin settings → Apps → AutoChase — same console for site admins
How to delegate: A site admin opens Access, searches for a person, and clicks Add global manager. Remove them anytime from the same table.

Products (on / off)

Under Access → Products, site admins choose what this installation uses. Turning a product off hides its pages and stops related scheduled work for everyone on the site.

ToggleWhen onWhen off
Capacity planner Capacity planning tab and capacity math are available. Capacity tab disappears. Capacity snapshots leave the issue panel.
SLA, reminders and escalations Dashboard, Tracked issues, Reports, SLA rules, reminders, escalations, and the issue SLA module. Those SLA pages hide. Escalation hierarchy hides under Organisation. The AutoChase SLA issue module is hidden on issues.
Keep at least one product on. You cannot turn off the last remaining product. Trial installs show a Trial badge on the Products card until the Marketplace trial ends.

What you see in the nav

Navigation adapts to the toggles:

  • Both on: Dashboard · Tracked issues · Reports · Capacity planning · Projects · SLA rules · Organisation · Access (admins)
  • Capacity only: Capacity planning · Projects · Organisation (Departments & Teams) · Access (admins)
  • SLA only: Dashboard · Tracked issues · Reports · Projects · SLA rules · Organisation (incl. Escalation hierarchy) · Access (admins)

Getting started

Install once, then follow the path that matches the products you enabled.

Install from the Marketplace

Choose AutoChase for Jira Cloud and complete the site install.

Open the console

Go to Apps → AutoChase. Site admins can also open it from Jira admin → Apps.

Set products & access

On Access, turn Capacity and/or SLA on, then add any global managers who should configure the app.

Add projects

Under Projects, load Jira projects, set owner email, optional default SLA and team, then sync issues if SLA is on.

Configure the products you enabled

SLA path: SLA rules → Organisation (incl. hierarchy) → review Dashboard. Capacity path: Setup → Availability → Sprint commitment / Backlog pressure.

If SLA is on

  • Create at least one SLA rule
  • Add department, team, and escalation hierarchy
  • Sync issues and confirm the issue panel on a sample ticket

If Capacity is on

  • Open Capacity → Setup and save estimate defaults
  • Add leave / meetings under Availability
  • Review Sprint commitment and Backlog pressure
When SLA is on, a setup reminder stays on Dashboard and Reports until department, team, and escalation hierarchy exist. Team reports and manager-chain escalations need that org data.

SLA rules

Define response and resolution targets, reminders, leadership visibility, clock behaviour, and working hours.

Modes

  • Single SLA — same targets for all priorities
  • Priority-based SLA (P1–P5) — different targets per priority

Five-step wizard

  1. SLA Timing — name, times, warning %, scope, clock, working hours
  2. Acknowledgment reminders — up to 3 % of response time
  3. Progress reminders — up to 3 % of resolution time
  4. Leadership visibility — manager-chain thresholds
  5. Review & submit
FieldPurpose
Response / Resolution timeSLA targets
Warning threshold (%)When status becomes At risk
Clock startsCreated, on status match, or on active sprint
Pause while status is…Statuses that stop the SLA clock
Working timeCount time only during business hours

Status lifecycle: On track → At risk (warning %) → Breach (resolution exceeded).

Working calendar

Control when SLA time counts — business hours, weekends, short days, holidays, and timezone.

Configure this in SLA rules → Create/Edit SLA → Step 1 → Working time.

  • Enable Count this SLA only during working hours
  • Pick an IANA timezone (or Use my timezone)
  • Set work days and start/end times; optionally different hours per day
  • Add holidays as dates, CSV/TSV/Excel, or use regional presets
Example: Mon–Thu 09:00–17:00, Friday 09:00–13:00, weekends off, plus holidays — a 16-hour SLA only burns business minutes.

Issue panel

Per-issue SLA (and capacity) context inside Jira. Shown when the SLA product is on.

Open a tracked issue and select the AutoChase SLA panel in the right rail.

SLA snapshot

  • On track / At risk / Breach
  • Escalation level & acknowledgment
  • Applied SLA and source (auto vs manual)
  • Time remaining and live progress

Capacity snapshot

  • Shown when Capacity is also on
  • Assignee workload vs weekly capacity
  • Capacity at risk vs SLA time left
  • Quick acknowledge / Assign SLA actions
When SLA, reminders and escalations is off, the entire AutoChase SLA issue module is hidden — not just emptied.

Availability

Setup (gear)

Team defaults for capacity math. Day-to-day leave and meetings belong in Availability. Product on/off lives under Access → Products, not here.

SettingDescription
Estimate fromRemaining estimate, Original estimate, or Story points
Minutes if missingFallback when an issue has no estimate (0 = exclude)
Story points field / Hours per pointConvert points to hours
Default hours / weekBaseline capacity per person
Meeting hours / Focus factorSimple meeting load and focused-work share
Work days / Hours per dayCalendar math for leave and sprint windows
Status rulesDone, handoff, and deferred statuses

Save settings, then Refresh on the relevant capacity tab.

How demand is calculated

  1. Uses Remaining / Original / Story points per Setup
  2. Applies per-project overrides when configured
  3. Falls back to minutes-if-missing or points × hours/point
  4. Status rules classify active, handoff, or deferred work
  5. Unassigned work can appear in a separate pool
Shared setup Projects, organisation, and end-to-end flow

Projects

Choose which Jira projects AutoChase tracks. Projects define both SLA scope and capacity scope.

  • Sync / Load projects — pull metadata from Jira
  • Sync issues — import issues into SLA tracking (when SLA is on)
  • Owner email (required) — escalation contact when tickets are unassigned
  • Issue types / Default SLA / Team — optional per-project controls

Add at least one project before running capacity or SLA reports.

Organisation

Structure people and teams for analytics and escalations.

Departments Teams Escalation hierarchy

Departments & Teams

Create departments first, then teams under them. Assign teams to projects from the Projects tab. Used by report filters and org columns.

Escalation hierarchy

Shown only when the SLA product is on. Upload the manager chain for leadership visibility.

  1. Download template (employee_id, name, email, reports_to_id)
  2. Import CSV, TSV, or Excel
  3. Click Resolve Jira IDs
  4. Verify the table; use Add employee for edits

SLA leadership visibility levels walk this chain from the assignee.

How it works

End-to-end flow when both products are enabled. Steps that need SLA or Capacity simply skip when that product is off.

  1. Tracking — New issues in enabled projects are tracked (webhook + sync)
  2. SLA assignment — Project default and matching rules; manual override per issue
  3. SLA clock — Runs in working hours if configured; pauses on selected statuses
  4. Status — on_track → at_risk → breach
  5. Reminders — Acknowledgment and progress comments at thresholds
  6. Leadership visibility — Escalation via hierarchy on scheduled checks
  7. Capacity — Open workload vs weekly/sprint supply and SLA time remaining
  8. Resolution — Tracking stops when the issue is done/resolved
